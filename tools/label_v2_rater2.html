Labeling guideline — read once before starting
  1. Pick the ONE mode that best explains the primary complaint. If two apply, pick the primary; note the other.
  2. Listing mismatch vs. performance shortfall: a physical attribute that differs from the listing (thread length, finish, quantity, geometry, size) is listing mismatch; a rating the part fails to perform to (load, pressure, capacity) is performance shortfall.
  3. Customer selection/misuse vs. performance shortfall: customer selection only when the reviewer does not fault the product, OR when the complaint is the product's defining, advertised behavior (foaming glue that foamed). If the reviewer blames the product otherwise, performance shortfall.
  4. Did anything break? Structural failure = functional failure. Falling short of a rating without breaking = performance shortfall.
  5. Thread damage vs. mismatch: binding, forcing, galling = damage. Loose, shallow, wobbly, wrong form = mismatch.
  6. Symptom vs. cause: follow the reviewer's own attribution. If they cite the listing/description, listing mismatch.
  7. Specific beats general: a specific mode (e.g. material nonconformance) beats listing mismatch even if the reviewer says "not as described."
  8. Wrong part = bag contents disagree with the bag's own label. Other/unclear only when genuinely vague.
  9. Prefix your note with [high], [medium] or [low] for how sure you are.
